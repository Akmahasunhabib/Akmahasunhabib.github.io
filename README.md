### About

I am a Ph.D. student in the Department of Mechanical and Aerospace Engineering at Texas Tech University (CGPA 3.956/4.00), working in the **M3D Lab** under Prof. Paul F. Egan. My research investigates multi-material lattice mechanics and bio-ink printability, integrating finite element methods, machine learning, and high-performance computing for materials optimization.

Before my Ph.D. I spent three years at **Avery Dennison** as a Global Product Development Officer, leading materials selection and property analysis for adhesive polymers, RFID inlays, and specialty coatings.

- 🎓 M.S. Mechanical Engineering, Texas Tech University (2026) · B.Sc. IPE, BUET
- 🔬 Currently: mixed unit-cell lattices for biomedical 3D printing, automated print-fidelity assessment
- 📍 Lubbock, Texas · 📫 akhabib@ttu.edu
